Delete Your LifeTask Account

You can permanently delete your LifeTask account and most associated content from inside the app.

Effective and last updated: July 24, 2026

On this page
  1. Before you delete
  2. Delete in the app
  3. What deletion removes

Before you delete

Deleting your LifeTask account does not cancel a subscription managed by Apple, Google, or Stripe. To prevent continued billing and preserve access to the account needed to manage a purchase, LifeTask requires you to cancel any actively renewing paid subscription before deletion.

  • Apple: open iPhone or iPad Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Google Play: open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions and Subscriptions.
  • Web billing: use Manage subscription in LifeTask to open the billing portal.

After canceling, reopen LifeTask and allow the updated subscription status to sync. If LifeTask still reports an active renewal, contact us and we will help verify it.

Delete in the app

If you cannot access the app, email dragos@trylifetask.com from the address associated with the account and use the subject “Account deletion request.” Never send us your password or full payment-card information. We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership.

  • Sign in to the LifeTask account you want to delete.
  • Open Account from the app navigation.
  • Choose Delete account and review the confirmation message.
  • Confirm permanent deletion.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes the main LifeTask account and most associated content, including profile information, reflections, learning progress, and generated-course records. This action is permanent and the deleted content generally cannot be recovered.

We may keep limited hashed identifiers, sanitized billing references, quota records, and deletion metadata when needed for fraud prevention, subscription recovery, support, disputes, accounting, or legal obligations. Apple, Google, Stripe, and other providers may retain transaction records under their own policies and legal duties.

Downloaded or cached course content may remain on your device until you remove it, the operating system clears it, or you uninstall LifeTask.

Need help?

Contact dragos@trylifetask.com. Include the email address used for LifeTask and a short description of the issue, but do not include passwords or full payment details.
